## 1. Security Guidelines

Doomsday Enterprise maintains strict security protocols to protect company and customer data.

### 1.1 Acceptable Use

All company systems must be used for legitimate business purposes only. Unauthorized use of company systems is a violation of this policy and may result in disciplinary action.

### 1.2 Data Protection

Employees handling Restricted or Confidential data must comply with the Data Classification policy outlined in the IT Access Policy.

---

## 2. Network Security

### 2.1 Firewall Rules

All inbound and outbound traffic is monitored and filtered by the corporate firewall. Employees must not attempt to bypass firewall rules or security controls.

### 2.2 Wireless Networks

Employees must only connect to approved corporate wireless networks. Public WiFi networks must not be used for accessing internal systems without VPN.

## 3. Security Training

### 3.1 Mandatory Training

All employees must complete the annual security awareness training within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Failure to complete training may result in account suspension.

### 3.2 Phishing Awareness

Employees must report suspicious emails to security@doomsday.example.com immediately. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.

---

## 4. Physical Security

### 4.1 Visitor Management

All visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge at all times. Employees must not leave visitors unattended in secure areas.

### 4.2 Clean Desk Policy

Employees must not leave sensitive documents or devices unattended. Lock workstations when stepping away from your desk.
