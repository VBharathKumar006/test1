# Nexora Technologies — HR Policy Handbook

## Version: 2.0
## Department: Human Resources
## Effective Date: January 1, 2024
## Document Status: Active

---

## 1. Annual Leave Policy

### 1.1 Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees of Nexora Technologies are entitled to **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, effective from the date of joining.

Part-time employees receive a pro-rated leave entitlement based on their contracted hours.

Employees who joined before January 1, 2024 and were covered under the previous policy (HR Policy v1.0) are subject to a transition period. Please contact HR for individual entitlement clarification.

### 1.2 Leave Accumulation

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following year, subject to a maximum carryover of 5 days. Any leave balance exceeding 5 days at the end of the calendar year will be forfeited.

### 1.3 Leave Application Process

1. Employees must submit leave requests through the HR Self-Service Portal at least 5 business days in advance.
2. Leave requests are subject to manager approval.
3. During critical project milestones, leave requests may be deferred at manager discretion.
4. Emergency leave of up to 2 days may be requested with 24 hours' notice.

---

## 2. Sick Leave Policy

### 2.1 Sick Leave Entitlement

All employees are entitled to **10 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not accumulate and unused days are not compensated.

### 2.2 Medical Certificate Requirements

A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. The certificate must be submitted to HR within 3 business days of returning to work.

---

## 3. Remote Work Policy

### 3.1 General Remote Work Guidelines

Nexora Technologies supports a hybrid work model. Employees are permitted to work remotely **up to 3 days per week**, subject to business requirements and manager approval.

### 3.2 Remote Work Equipment

The company provides a laptop and relevant software licences for all employees. Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a suitable work environment and internet connection.

### 3.3 Core Hours

All employees, regardless of work location, are expected to be available during core hours of **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time**.

---

## 4. Probation Policy

### 4.1 Probation Period

New employees serve a probation period of **90 days**. During probation, either party may terminate employment with 7 days' notice.

### 4.2 Performance Review

A formal performance review is conducted at the end of the probation period. Successful completion results in confirmation of employment.

---

## 5. Performance Review Cycle

Performance reviews are conducted **twice per year**: mid-year in June and year-end in December.

---

## 6. Code of Conduct

### 6.1 Professional Behaviour

All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally and respectfully in all workplace interactions.

### 6.2 Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client data, or proprietary processes to unauthorized parties.
