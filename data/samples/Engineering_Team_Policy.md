# Nexora Technologies — Engineering Team Policy

## Version: 1.2
## Department: Engineering
## Team: Engineering (All Sub-teams)
## Effective Date: June 1, 2023
## Document Status: Active

---

## 1. Engineering Remote Work Policy

### 1.1 Remote Work Entitlement

Engineering team members are permitted to work remotely **up to 2 days per week**. This is a team-specific policy that differs from the company-wide 3-day remote work allowance to ensure sufficient in-person collaboration during sprint planning and architecture reviews.

### 1.2 Mandatory On-site Days

Engineering team members must be present in the office on **Tuesdays and Thursdays**. These are designated collaboration days.

### 1.3 Stand-up Attendance

All Engineering team members must attend the daily stand-up meeting at **9:00 AM** in-person when on-site or via video conference when remote.

---

## 2. Engineering Leave Policy

### 2.1 Additional Leave Entitlement

Engineering employees are entitled to **5 additional leave days** per year as a technical wellness benefit, in addition to the standard company-wide annual leave entitlement. This brings total annual leave for Engineering employees to **25 days per year** (20 company-wide + 5 engineering-specific).

### 2.2 Leave During Sprint

Leave during the final week of a sprint (sprint closure week) requires additional approval from the Engineering Manager and must be communicated at least 2 weeks in advance.

---

## 3. On-Call Policy

### 3.1 On-Call Rotation

Senior engineers participate in a 24/7 on-call rotation. Engineers are on-call for one week at a time, with rotations scheduled quarterly.

### 3.2 On-Call Compensation

Engineers who respond to on-call incidents outside working hours receive:
- 1 additional day off per on-call week
- Incident response time added to overtime records

---

## 4. Development Standards

### 4.1 Code Review Requirements

All code changes must be reviewed by at least **2 engineers** before merging to the main branch. Pull requests must include:
- Adequate test coverage (minimum 80% code coverage for new features)
- Updated documentation
- No failing tests

### 4.2 Release Approvals

Production releases require sign-off from the Engineering Manager and QA Lead.

---

## 5. Software and Tools

Engineering employees may request access to development tools and software through the IT Service Portal. Standard engineering tools (IDE licences, GitHub, Jira, Confluence) are pre-approved for all Engineering team members.

---

## 6. Password Policy Exception

For engineering development and testing environments, the standard 180-day password rotation policy applies. However, service account credentials used in CI/CD pipelines must be rotated every **90 days** and stored in the approved secrets management vault.
