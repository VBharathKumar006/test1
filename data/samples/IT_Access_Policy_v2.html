IT Access and Security Policy

Version: 2.1 | Department: IT | Effective Date: March 1, 2024

1. Account Access Management

1.1 New Account Requests

All requests for system access must be submitted through the IT Service Portal at portal.doomsday.example.com. Access requests must be approved by the employee's direct manager before IT can provision the account.

1.2 VPN Access

Employees must submit VPN access requests through the IT service portal. VPN access is granted to employees who require remote access to internal systems. The approval process typically takes 2 business days.

1.3 Privileged Access

Administrative and privileged system access requires additional authorization from the IT Security team and the relevant department head. Such requests must include a business justification and are reviewed quarterly.

2. Password Policy

2.1 Password Rotation Requirements

All employee passwords must be changed every 180 days. The system will prompt users automatically when rotation is due.

2.2 Password Complexity

  • Minimum 12 characters
  • Must include uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Must include at least one number
  • Must include at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
  • Cannot reuse the last 10 passwords

2.3 Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is mandatory for all employee accounts. Employees must enroll in MFA within 5 business days of account creation.

3. Device Management

3.1 Approved Devices

Only company-issued devices may be used to access internal systems and confidential data. Personal devices are not permitted to access corporate networks without explicit IT approval.

3.2 Mobile Device Management

All company-issued mobile devices must be enrolled in the Doomsday Mobile Device Management (MDM) system.

4. Data Classification

Classification Level Description Handling Requirements
Public Information safe for external distribution No special restrictions
Internal General company information Internal employees only
Confidential Sensitive business data Need-to-know basis, encrypted storage
Restricted Highly sensitive or regulated data Strict access controls, audit logging required

5. Incident Reporting

Security incidents, including suspected data breaches, unauthorized access attempts, or device loss, must be reported to the IT Security team within 1 hour of discovery. Report incidents via security@doomsday.example.com or the IT Service Portal.

6. Software Installation

Only software approved by the IT department may be installed on company devices. Employees must not install unauthorized software, browser extensions, or plugins. Submit software requests through the IT Service Portal.

For IT support: support@doomsday.example.com | Service Portal: portal.doomsday.example.com
