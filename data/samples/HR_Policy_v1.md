# Nexora Technologies — HR Policy Handbook

## Version: 1.0
## Department: Human Resources
## Effective Date: March 15, 2022
## Document Status: Superseded (see HR Policy v2.0)

---

## 1. Annual Leave Policy

### 1.1 Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees of Nexora Technologies are entitled to **18 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year.

### 1.2 Leave Accumulation

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following year, up to a maximum of 3 days.

---

## 2. Sick Leave Policy

All employees are entitled to **8 days of paid sick leave** per year.

---

## 3. Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.

---

## 4. Probation Policy

New employees serve a probation period of **60 days**.

---
