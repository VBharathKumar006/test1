DOOMSDAY ENTERPRISE — FINANCE AND REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Version: 3.0
Department: Finance
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Document Status: Active

=============================================================
1. TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT
=============================================================

1.1 Domestic Travel

Employees may claim reimbursement for domestic business travel expenses
up to the following limits:

  - Economy flights: Actual cost (no limit, economy class only)
  - Hotel accommodation: Maximum $150 per night
  - Daily meal allowance: $50 per day
  - Ground transportation: Actual cost with receipts
  - Mileage (personal vehicle): $0.67 per mile

All claims must be submitted within 30 days of travel via the Finance
Portal at finance.doomsday.example.com.

1.2 International Travel

International travel reimbursement limits are higher to account for
cost-of-living variations:

  - Business class flights: Permitted for flights exceeding 8 hours
  - Economy class flights: Required for flights under 8 hours
  - Hotel accommodation: Maximum $250 per night
  - Daily meal allowance: $80 per day
  - Ground transportation: Actual cost with receipts

All international travel requires pre-approval from the department head
and Finance team before booking.

1.3 Expenses Not Eligible for Reimbursement

The following expenses are NOT reimbursable:
  - Alcohol and entertainment for personal purposes
  - Minibar charges
  - Personal items and clothing
  - Speeding fines or parking violations
  - Travel insurance for personal travel

=============================================================
2. EQUIPMENT REIMBURSEMENT
=============================================================

2.1 Home Office Equipment

Employees approved for permanent remote work may claim reimbursement
for home office equipment up to $500 per year. This includes:
  - Monitor
  - Keyboard and mouse
  - Desk accessories
  - Ergonomic equipment

Receipts are required for all claims.

2.2 Mobile Phone Reimbursement

Employees who use personal mobile phones for work purposes may claim
up to $50 per month for business phone usage.

=============================================================
3. TRAINING AND CERTIFICATION REIMBURSEMENT
=============================================================

Doomsday Enterprise reimburses employees for approved professional
development activities:

  - Professional certifications: Up to $2,000 per year
  - Online courses and training: Up to $500 per year
  - Industry conferences: Up to $3,000 per year (requires manager approval)

Employees must remain with the company for at least 6 months following
certification reimbursement. If an employee leaves before this period,
50% of the reimbursement may be clawed back.

=============================================================
4. EXPENSE SUBMISSION PROCESS
=============================================================

1. Submit all expense claims through the Finance Portal within 30 days.
2. Attach all receipts (PDF or clear photograph).
3. Obtain manager approval in the portal.
4. Finance processes approved claims within 10 business days.
5. Reimbursements are made via payroll in the next pay cycle.

Claims submitted after 30 days will not be processed without a
written exception request approved by the Finance Director.

=============================================================
5. ADVANCE PAYMENTS
=============================================================

Employees may request a travel advance for international travel
expenses exceeding $1,000. Advance requests must be submitted at
least 5 business days before travel. Unused advances must be
returned within 5 business days of returning from travel.
