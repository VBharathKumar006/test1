1. ACCOUNT ACCESS MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

1.1 New Account Requests
All access requests must be submitted to IT Helpdesk via email at
helpdesk@doomsday.example.com. Approvals are provided within 5 business days.

1.2 VPN Access
VPN access requires manager approval via email submission to IT Helpdesk.

=============================================================

2. PASSWORD POLICY
=============================================================

2.1 Password Rotation
All passwords must be changed every 90 days. Failure to comply will result
in automatic account lockout.

2.2 Password Complexity
- Minimum 8 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter
- Must include at least one number

2.3 Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is recommended but optional for non-administrative accounts.

=============================================================

3. DEVICE MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

Only company-issued laptops may access internal systems.

=============================================================

4. INCIDENT REPORTING
=============================================================

Security incidents must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.
Contact: security@doomsday.example.com
